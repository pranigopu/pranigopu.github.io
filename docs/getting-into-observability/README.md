# About
Observability is the ability to understand the internal state or condition of a complex system based solely on knowledge of its external outputs; in practice, external outputs often refer to the system's telemetry. The pursuit of observability involves the creation/maintenance of procedures, workflows, and infrastructure that facilitate both data collection and data inference.

> **Reference**: [What is observability?, **www.ibm.com/think/topics/observability**](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/observability)

# Approach
I want to clarity my approach to engaging with topics in observability in particular and technical topics in general, because it will help myself and you, the reader, understand how to structure our reading to get the most value and meaning out of the technical material. My approach involves the differentiation of 2 aspects of a study:

1. Design focus
2. Technical focus

Design focus refers to the focus of principles, intent, and wide/diverse implications. In other words, it is focus on the way in which something offers a paradigm, pattern, or framework for dealing with a situation/problem-statement that is described in abstract terms (i.e. in terms that refer to an unlimited class of situations/problem-statements, not just a specific scenario). Technical focus, on the other hand, refers to the focus on usage, implementation, and maintenance. In other words, it is focus on the way in which something offers a concrete tool, application, or infrastructure for dealing with a situation/problem-statement that can be broad or specific, but that has specific parameters that can be addressed by specific tools/applications/infrastructure.

I believe no topic is purely one or the other. Nonetheless, there is always some focus, or "bent", toward one side or the other. Both sides are important. Design focus engages conceptual reasoning, serving to empower oneself more generally. Technical focus engages technical know-how and actionable information, serving to empower oneself more specifically. Without design focus, every problem becomes wholly novel and thus destabilising. Without technical focus, actual practice falters and fails.

My ultimate goal is empower myself more generally, and use specific knowledge and skills to build myself up as is suitable. In other words, I seek design focus grounded in technical focus where needed. To help myself in my pursuit, I shall mark documents where possible as **[TECHNICAL FOCUS]** and **[DESIGN FOCUS]**. You may be surprised with what I choose to mark one way or the other!
