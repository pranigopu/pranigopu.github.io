<h1>Statement Of Motivation</h1>

---

Since motivation shapes direction and drive, let me make my own motivation clear about this project. Part of it is the desire to ensure my efforts a outputs are not lost to proprietary history. But there is more to it than the desire to document and make visible, because I do not just want to present but to integrate, explain at more abstract, broader levels, to, in a real sense, find meaning in the part of my life that went into this project.

Where does this drive come from? From the conviction that life as such has no meaning, no justifying narrative, nothing that is meant to persist. However, I cannot escape the fact that life does have meaning, in its details, in its base fact, in the first-hand experience of it. Values are an emergent property of the same structures that give rise to a given form of life, not universal or "objective" in some independent way, but real, even urgent, given the fragility of life.

Thus, if meaning lies in the details, if values emerge from life, and - here is the new detail - if my being strives for life, thus striving toward the values that emerge from it and sustain its shape and substance, it follows that I engage with these details to integrate them to my life and values. This is why I'm not satisfied with a technical work, or even systems design. I want to engage with that which has consistently tied the details of my life to principles, values, and the broader con of my life: philosophy.
